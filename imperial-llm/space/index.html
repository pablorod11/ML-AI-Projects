What this establishes

Tokenization, causal attention, gradient descent, serialization and browser generation work together. A tiny corpus and small model do not establish general language ability.

Model notes

The classic Transformer architecture comes from Fareed Khan’s open training project. We train a compact character model, then execute its operations directly in JavaScript.

  1. Characters become numbers

    A corpus-derived character vocabulary maps your text to token IDs. Unsupported characters are reported explicitly.

  2. Attention reads the context

    Learned token and position embeddings pass through causal multi-head attention, layer normalization, residual connections and ReLU feed-forward layers.

  3. One more character

    The output layer scores the vocabulary. Temperature and top-k sampling select the next character. The context window advances as the text grows.
